Standard Response Protocol

Hold: In your room or area. Clear the halls.

Secure: Get inside. Lock outside doors.

Lockdown: Locks, lights, out of sight.

Evacuate: Move to the announced location.

Shelter: Follow the announced hazard strategy.

When to Report

Report safety concerns, threats, suspicious activity, weapons, dangerous behavior, or other information that may affect student or campus safety.
